Pumpkin Banana Muffins

Ingredients — makes 12

No baking soda? Use 1 tablespoon (3 tsp) baking powder instead of the 1 tsp baking soda below.
  • 1½ cupAll-purpose flour
  • 1 tspBaking soda (or 1 tbsp baking powder)
  • ½ tspSalt
  • 1½ tspPumpkin pie spice (or cinnamon)
  • 2Ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 cupCanned pumpkin puree
  • ½ cupGranulated sugar
  • ⅓ cupVegetable oil or melted butter
  • 2Large eggs
  • 1 tspVanilla extract
  • ½ cupChocolate chips or walnuts (optional)
