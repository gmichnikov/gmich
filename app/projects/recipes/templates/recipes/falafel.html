Falafel

Ingredients — makes 12–15

Using canned chickpeas? Drain and pat dry thoroughly. Add an extra tablespoon of flour to help bind.
  • 1 cupDried chickpeas, soaked overnight (or 1 can, drained)
  • ½ cupFresh parsley, packed
  • ½ cupFresh cilantro, packed
  • ¼ cupOnion, roughly chopped
  • 4Garlic cloves
  • 1 tspGround cumin
  • 1 tspGround coriander
  • ¼ tspCayenne pepper (optional)
  • 1 tspSalt
  • ¼ tspBlack pepper
  • 2 tbspChickpea flour (or all-purpose)
  • ½ tspBaking powder
  • —Oil for frying
