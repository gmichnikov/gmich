Install on Android

SS to Cal must be installed to your home screen before it appears in the share sheet.

  1. Open this page in Chrome on Android and log in with Google.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu → Add to Home screen → confirm.
  3. Open SS to Cal from your home screen (standalone — no URL bar).
  4. Take a screenshot, tap Share, and choose SS to Cal.
  5. If SS to Cal does not appear in the share list, force-close Chrome and try again.

Ready to use

Take a screenshot of anything with event info, tap Share, choose SS to Cal, verify the details, and add to Google Calendar.
