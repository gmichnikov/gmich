Install on Android

Share sheet integration only works after SS to Cal is installed to your home screen.

  1. Open this page in Chrome on Android.
  2. Log in with Google if prompted.
  3. Tap the three-dot menu → Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm the install. SS to Cal opens standalone (no URL bar).
  5. Test: take a screenshot, tap Share, and look for SS to Cal in the list. Restart Chrome once if it does not appear.

Ready to use

Take a screenshot of anything with event info, tap Share, choose SS to Cal, verify the details, and add to Google Calendar.
