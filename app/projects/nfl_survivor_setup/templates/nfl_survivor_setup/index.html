NFL Survivor

Setup and Rules

How to create an account, join the pool, and how the rules work.

Steps for creating an account

  1. Go to https://www.gregmichnikov.com/register
  2. Either register using an email and password, or choose Login with Google.
    1. If you registered via email, look for a verification email in your inbox and click the Verify button. Then log in using your password.
  3. Click NFL Survivor (the first thing on the page when you log in) or go to https://www.gregmichnikov.com/nfl-survivor/
  4. Give your Survivor Pool entry a name.
  5. Make your Week 1 pick.

If you want an additional entry, for yourself or a kid, click the football with the + sign at the top right. No need for an additional account.

Rules

  • As many entries as you want. Children and any other new participants are welcome.
  • Double elimination. Ties count as wins.
  • Multiple picks may be required the last few weeks, depending on how many people are left.
  • Picks are due at the time of the game you are picking.
